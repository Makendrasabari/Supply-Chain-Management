OUR STORY

FROM   THREE   SPREADSHEETS
TO   ONE   PLAN.

In 2018 our founders were shift managers at a Frankfurt cross-dock. Every morning began with a phone call to reconcile stock counts between the ERP, the carrier portal and the warehouse floor.

Today Stackly sits between your ERP, your carriers and your warehouses, and turns their separate feeds into a single, live plan for every planner in the company.

  • Connects to SAP, Oracle, NetSuite and EDI in under two weeks
  • SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified
  • A named logistics analyst on every account

WHAT WE VALUE

THREE   RULES   WE   PLAN   BY.

Every product decision at Stackly is tested against these.

Trust the floor

If the data disagrees with the people on the dock, the data is wrong. We fix it at the source.

Show the delay early

A late alert is a useless alert. We surface risk while there is still time to act.

Plain answers

No jargon dashboards. Every screen answers one question a planner actually asks.

MILESTONES

EIGHT   YEARS,   ONE   SCREEN.

2018 · The first pallet count

Two shift managers build a shared stock sheet that finally matches the floor.

Eliminated daily morning inventory disputes across dock doors. Provided the single verifiable truth that became the foundation for Stackly.

2020 · First 10 carriers

Live ocean and road tracking goes into production at three retailers.

Connected disparate EDI feeds into continuous real-time milestone streams. Slashed manual dispatch check-calls by sixty percent within sixty days.

2022 · Forecasting launches

Weekly SKU forecasts that learn from promotions and lead-time drift.

Integrated historical supplier variance to forecast delays before vessels depart. Enabled planners to balance safety stock buffers weeks ahead of peak.

2024 · 62 countries

Regional teams in Rotterdam, Singapore and Chicago join the network.

Expanded across intercontinental corridors with automated customs hold alerts. Allowed multinational freight desks to orchestrate handoffs in real time.

2026 · Freight audit

Automatic invoice matching pays for the platform inside one quarter.

Reconciles rate sheets, detention fees and accessorial surcharges automatically. Recovers hidden carrier billing overcharges directly to the operating margin.
